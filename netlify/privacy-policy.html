Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2024

1. Information We Collect

Motomastar is designed to help garage and workshop owners manage their daily operations. To provide these services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Workshop name, owner name, contact number, and email address.
  • Customer Data: Details of customers, including their names, contact details, and vehicle information (such as RC details).
  • Operational Data: Job cards, bookings, inventory records, staff details, revenue, and payment transactions entered into the system.
  • Media: Images (such as vehicle photos or receipts) uploaded by the user.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the purpose of providing, improving, and maintaining the Motomastar application services. This includes:

  • Managing workshop job cards, staff performance, and customer bookings.
  • Generating operational reports and analytics.
  • Providing customer support and communicating service updates.

3. Data Sharing and Security

Your data is securely stored and never shared with or sold to third-party marketing companies. Data is only accessible to authorized personnel of the workshop through role-based access controls within the app.

4. Data Retention

We retain operational and customer data as long as your Motomastar account is active. If you wish to delete your data or account, please follow the procedure outlined on our Delete Account page.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@Motomastar.app.
